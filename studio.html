The team’s story began in 2023 with Borsyq Games, an indie studio that grew out of the GameDev Center incubation program.

In 2023, the team went through incubation, tested its first game and digital formats, and entered industry platforms. In 2026, a new chapter received the name SeeVerse Studio: original development joined community curation, internships, educational programs and major industry events.
