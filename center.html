GameDev Center
Astana Hub

Not a company or a closed club, but an open meeting point for the games industry: education, practice, teams, partners and opportunities.

The SeeVerse team curates the community, brings emerging and experienced developers together, and runs game jams, industry zones and educational programs. The Center grew from the CG community and OMNIUM initiatives, preserving continuity and passing knowledge forward.
