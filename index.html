01 / STUDIO

MORE THAN DEVELOPMENT

A WORLD WHERE
GAMES BECOME
A MEETING POINT.

The team’s story began in 2023 with Borsyq Games, an indie studio that grew out of the GameDev Center incubation program.

In 2023, the team went through incubation, tested its first game and digital formats, and entered industry platforms. In 2026, a new chapter received the name SeeVerse Studio: original development joined community curation, internships, educational programs and major industry events.

CURATED BY THE SEEVERSE TEAM

GameDev Center
Astana Hub

Not a company or a closed club, but an open meeting point for the games industry: education, practice, teams, partners and opportunities.

The SeeVerse team curates the community, brings emerging and experienced developers together, and runs game jams, industry zones and educational programs. The Center grew from the CG community and OMNIUM initiatives, preserving continuity and passing knowledge forward.
